# rust_gomoku Android Privacy Policy

**Developer** · chubbyk-uu

rust_gomoku is an offline Gomoku game. The Android app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data or gameplay data. It has no user accounts, ads, or analytics.

The app stores the interface-language preference and bundled engine models on your device so it can run offline. You can remove those files and preferences using **Clear storage** in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

If you open a project link from the app, your browser handles that visit. External sites and Google Play may handle data under their own policies.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the project's GitHub issue tracker. The tracker is public, so leave out personal information. [Contact the developer](https://github.com/chubbyk-uu/rust-gomoku/issues/new)

## rust_gomoku Android 隐私政策

**开发者** · chubbyk-uu

rust_gomoku 是一款离线五子棋游戏。Android 应用不收集、传输、出售或共享个人数据和对局数据，也没有用户账号、广告或统计分析功能。

应用在本机保存界面语言偏好及内置引擎模型，以便离线运行。清除 Android 应用存储或卸载应用即可删除这些文件和偏好。

打开应用里的项目链接后，由浏览器处理访问。外部网站和 Google Play 可能依据各自的政策处理数据。

如有隐私问题，请通过项目的 GitHub 问题区联系开发者。问题区公开可见，请勿填写个人信息。 [联系开发者](https://github.com/chubbyk-uu/rust-gomoku/issues/new)
